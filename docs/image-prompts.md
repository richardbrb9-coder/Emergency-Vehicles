# Image prompts (Nano Banana / Gemini)

The interactive parts (vehicles, Pip, crew, doors, flames, ladders) are drawn in code so they can move.
Use generated images only for the items below. Paste the **style block** at the start of every prompt.

**Style block**
> Flat vector cartoon illustration for a children's app aged 4–6. Thick dark navy outlines, rounded shapes, bright flat colours, soft simple shading, friendly and calm. British setting. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

## 1. App icon (square, 1024x1024)
Style block + "A happy Dalmatian puppy wearing a hi-vis yellow vest and a small red cap, peeking over the side of a red fire engine, on a bright blue circle background. Simple, bold, readable at small sizes."

## 2. Splash / loading picture (16:9, 2560x1440)
Style block + "A cheerful British rescue station at sunrise with a fire engine, ambulance and police car parked in front, a Dalmatian puppy in a hi-vis vest waving. Leave the top third as plain sky."

## 3. Colouring pages (one per vehicle, 4:3, 2048x1536)
"Black and white colouring page for a 4 year old. Thick clean black outlines only, no shading, no grey, white background, no text. A [UK fire engine / ambulance / police car / lifeboat / air ambulance helicopter / hovercraft / rotator tow truck / helicopter with water bucket / race track medical car] seen from the side, large and centred."

## 4. Red word pictures (square, 1024x1024, plain white background)
Style block + "A single [hydrant / nozzle / stretcher / helipad / winch / life jacket / siren / paramedic / helmet / radio] on a plain white background, centred, large, nothing else in the picture."

## 5. Optional richer scene backgrounds (16:9, 2560x1440)
Only if you prefer a more painted look than the current flat backgrounds. Each must be empty of vehicles and people, with the bottom 10% as road and the top 20% as plain sky.
- Maple Street: "A quiet British street of terraced houses with a pavement and road, a tree on the right."
- Muddy beach: "A wide muddy British beach at low tide with the sea in the distance."
- Motorway: "A British motorway with crash barriers and green fields."
- Forest hills: "Tall pine forest on hills under a blue sky."
- Race circuit: "A motor racing circuit with red and white kerbs and grandstands."
- Harbour: "A small British harbour with a lifeboat slipway and calm sea."
- Hospital roof: "A hospital rooftop helipad with a big painted circle, city behind."

Save images as PNG into `app/images/` using the names in brackets, e.g. `app/images/colouring-fire.png`.
