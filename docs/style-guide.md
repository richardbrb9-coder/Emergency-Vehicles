# To the Rescue! — Style guide

## Look
- Flat vector cartoon. Thick navy outline (#2a2f45, 5px at 1600x900 scene size), rounded joins, no gradients except sky and water.
- Bright, saturated colours. Every vehicle is drawn side-on facing right, sitting on a soft shadow.
- No text inside pictures, except short signs that are part of the world (RESCUE STATION, house numbers).
- Keep the top 170px of every 1600x900 scene as clear sky. Pip and the speech bubble live there.
- Keep anything a child must tap inside x 200–1400, so it still shows on 4:3 iPads.

## Type
- Reading text: Andika (made for early readers), bold, at least 3.6% of screen height.
- Titles and numbers: Fredoka.

## Voice and words
- Pip speaks every instruction. One short sentence at a time, present tense, "tap" not "click".
- Never "wrong". Use "Nearly! Try another one."
- UK words: lorry, pavement, 999, nee-naw.
- No real brand names (NHS, RNLI, Mercedes, F1).

## Motion
- Everything a child taps answers with a sound, a movement and a sparkle.
- Lights flash at 2 per second. In calm mode they glow slowly instead.
- Respect "reduce motion" on the device.
